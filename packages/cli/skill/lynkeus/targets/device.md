# Simulator or emulator

The app attached on the port is the target. Frames, films and `os` commands go to the device that app runs on (the one where its process is, of its platform), so a Simulator booted beside the emulator does not get in the way. `os language <tag>` relaunches it in a language, which every later relaunch Lynkeus makes keeps. On Android, `status` runs `adb reverse` for the port. Lynkeus never boots or closes a device: shut down what you booted, never what was already running.
