# A text does not match, or a wait times out on a screen that is there

- A paragraph matches only by its whole text: `app.see` with `contains: true`. A capitalized bare word is a route (`'"Next"'` is the text). `why` names the closest text on screen, or, for a renamed control (`Log Out` to `Sign Out`), the new control of the same kind, nearest to the old place.
- A route name comes from the navigation adapter: `screen` shows the real one.
